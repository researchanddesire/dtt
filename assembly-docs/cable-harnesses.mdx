---
title: Cable Harnesses
---

The harness connects the screen, sensor, battery, and PCB. Follow the routing
in the [assembly guide](/dtt/assembly-guide).

The [device BOM](https://github.com/researchanddesire/DT_Trainer-OSS/blob/main/hardware/bom.csv)
lists the harness as one assembly. Cable definitions belong in
[`hardware/cables/`](https://github.com/researchanddesire/DT_Trainer-OSS/tree/main/hardware/cables/).
